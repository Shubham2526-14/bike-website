About Ladakh Escapade

Is Not a Tour.
This Is an Escapade

At Ladakh Escapade Adventure Unlimited, we’re not just running a travel company — we’re living our dream and sharings it with you. What started as a passion for bikes and the mountains has turned into a way to help others experience the real Ladakh. We take Indian and international travellers on unforgettable journeys through rugged terrains, peaceful valleys, and offbeat routes most tourists miss. Our trips are all about good vibes, great company, and exploring Ladakh the way it’s meant to be — raw, beautiful, and full of adventure. With local guides who know the land inside out and bikes that are always ready for the road, we make sure your ride is safe, exciting, and truly memorable. Let’s ride together and make stories worth telling.

FAQ's

Some frequently asked Questions

Do I need prior biking experience to join the trip?

Not necessarily! If you know how to ride a geared bike and are comfortable on the road, you’re good to go. Our trips are beginner-friendly, and our experienced guides are there to support you at every stage of the journey.

Can foreign tourists join your trips?

Yes, definitely! We welcome riders from all over the world. Our team helps with all necessary travel permits and provides guidance to ensure a smooth and safe experience for international travelers..

Is the trip safe for solo travelers?

Yes! Solo riders are always welcome. You’ll join a guided group, so there’s a friendly vibe and plenty of support. Our team ensures you feel safe, connected, and well taken care of throughout the journey.

Do I need a special permit to travel in Ladakh?

Yes, some areas like Nubra Valley, Pangong Lake, and Umling La require special permits — ILP for Indians and PAP for foreigners. These are important due to the region's sensitive borders. But no worries — we’ll take care of all the permit arrangements for you before the trip.
