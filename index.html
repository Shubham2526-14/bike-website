About Ladakh Escapade

Is Not a Tour.
This Is an Escapade

At Ladakh Escapade Adventure Unlimited, we’re not just running a travel company — we’re living our dream and sharings it with you. What started as a passion for bikes and the mountains has turned into a way to help others experience the real Ladakh. We take Indian and international travellers on unforgettable journeys through rugged terrains, peaceful valleys, and offbeat routes most tourists miss. Our trips are all about good vibes, great company, and exploring Ladakh the way it’s meant to be — raw, beautiful, and full of adventure. With local guides who know the land inside out and bikes that are always ready for the road, we make sure your ride is safe, exciting, and truly memorable. Let’s ride together and make stories worth telling.

Why Ride With Us?

Expert Local
Guides

Our guides aren’t just trained professionals — they’re locals who’ve lived and breathed Ladakh their entire lives. They know the region like the back of their hand, from secret scenic routes to cultural gems most tourists miss. With them, every ride turns into a deeper experience filled with stories, legends, and real local flavor.

Safety Comes
First

We believe adventure should never come at the cost of safety. That’s why we follow strict safety protocols, regularly service our bikes, and provide backup support on all trips. Whether you're a first-time rider or a seasoned pro, we ensure a smooth, secure journey so you can focus on enjoying the ride.

Personalized
Experiences

No two riders are the same — and neither are our tours. We listen to your preferences and customize your trip to match your pace, interests, and comfort level. Whether you want a peaceful scenic ride or a hardcore off-road challenge, we make it your adventure.

Trusted by Global
Travelers

We’ve hosted riders from across India and around the world — and they keep coming back. From clear communication to helpful support with permits, stays, and logistics, we make sure international and domestic travelers feel right at home while exploring one of the most remote places on earth.
